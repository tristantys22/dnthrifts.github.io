Quick answers

How do I pay?

Choose PayLah! (scan the QR on checkout) or pay by card via Stripe — both are on the checkout page. Enter the amount shown, then DM us your receipt on Instagram to confirm.

Do you do meetups or delivery?

Both! We can meetup around NUS / Raffles Hall, or arrange delivery. DM us to sort out the details after payment.

Can I reserve an item?

We don't officially hold items, but drop us a DM and we'll do our best. First come first served though!

When's the next drop?

Follow us on Instagram @tristantys — we announce all new drops there first. Sometimes we drop some stuff, sometimes we don't. Stay tuned!

Are items washed before selling?

Yes — every item is washed and checked before it goes up. We wouldn't sell something we wouldn't wear ourselves.

Can I return an item?

We don't accept returns since items are pre-loved, but if there's a genuine issue with what you received, DM us and we'll work something out.
